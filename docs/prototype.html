What makes it different

We redesigned
how reading works

Most e-readers simply move a paper book onto a screen. We started somewhere else — with what only a screen can do.

Pillar 01

Four books open
on one desk

A textbook spread in front of you, references propped up beside it — studying has always worked this way. Keep one book open wide and three standing behind it, then pick any of them up and swap places whenever you like.

🖐 Pick a book up, drop it anywhere
Pillar 02

Pages that follow
your hand

Not a button that swaps one screen for another. Take hold of the corner and the page lifts as far as you pull it. Let go halfway and it settles back; carry it over and the shadow travels across the paper with it.

✋ Grab the corner and pull
Pillar 03

An AI that reads
your desk, not a page

Every other reader's assistant answers from inside the one book you have open. Ours sees all four at once. "How do these four explain the same idea differently?" — that question becomes askable for the first time.
